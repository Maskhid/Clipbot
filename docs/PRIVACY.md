# Privacy Policy — ClipBot

_Last updated: 4 November 2026_

ClipBot is a private tool operated by Abdulhameed Abdulmalik ("I") to publish my own video clips to social media accounts that I own, and to measure how those videos perform. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

## YouTube API Services

ClipBot uses the **YouTube API Services** to upload videos to my own YouTube channels and to read statistics about those channels and videos.

- By using ClipBot's YouTube features, users agree to be bound by the **YouTube Terms of Service**: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms
- YouTube data is also handled under the **Google Privacy Policy**: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## What information is accessed

When a YouTube channel (or a TikTok, Instagram or Facebook account) is connected, ClipBot receives an OAuth access token from that platform. With it, ClipBot accesses only:

- the connected channel's name and subscriber count
- the list of that channel's own recent videos, with their view, like and comment counts
- permission to upload new videos to that channel

ClipBot does **not** access other users' data, private messages, comments' authors, or any data about viewers.

## How the information is used

- Tokens are used only to upload videos that I have chosen to my own channels.
- Video statistics are used only to produce a private performance report for me.
- No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

## Storage and security

- OAuth tokens are stored **encrypted** (Fernet / AES) and can only be decrypted with a private key held as a secret.
- Video IDs and statistics for my own videos are stored in a private record.

## Retention and deletion

- Video statistics are kept for at most **90 days** and are then deleted automatically.
- Stored YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days, as the YouTube API Services Developer Policies require.
- Access can be revoked at any time through the **Google security settings page**: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. Revoking access makes the stored token useless, and its data is deleted at the next update.
- To request deletion of any stored data, email peaktheclipper@gmail.com. It will be deleted within 7 days.

## Cookies

ClipBot does not use cookies and does not place any information on users' devices.
